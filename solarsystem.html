What is Solar System?

The collection of eight planets and their moons in orbit round the sun, together with smaller bodies in the form of asteroids, meteoroids, and comets. The planets of the solar system are (in order of distance from the sun) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Solar System is not only of planets and moons we do have dwarf planets like pluto,huamea,makemake,eris,ceres.
